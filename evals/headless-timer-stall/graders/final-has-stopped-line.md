type: regex
pattern: 'Consultation stopped:[\s\S]*process tree ended'
---

The stop line is in the final answer at all — every field of the line is `stopped-line`'s job.
Markdown emphasis around the fixed words is fine, and so is the model's own answer written below
the line; a reply without the line, or one that ends `process tree NOT confirmed — pids …`,
fails.
